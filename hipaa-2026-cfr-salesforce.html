HIPAA Security Rule proposal · not in force

What's on the table for July 2027.

HHS published this proposal on January 6, 2025. The regulatory agenda targets final action in July 2027. That date is a planning target. It is not an effective date, and it is not a compliance date. The Security Rule in force today is unchanged. If a final rule is published, an effective date follows publication, and the compliance period generally cannot be shorter than 180 days. Specifications that are addressable today would become required, with limited exceptions. In the proposal, and not a Salesforce setting.

These would sit with your security program. Work Salesforce does inside its own infrastructure stays on Salesforce's side of the shared-responsibility model. It does not segment your network, scan your workstations, or write your risk analysis.

  • Anti-malware, removal of extraneous software, and disabling network ports according to your risk analysis.
  • Network segmentation.
  • Vulnerability scanning at least every 6 months, and penetration testing at least every 12 months. Health Check and Security Center do not run those tests.
  • The written risk analysis itself: the inventory, the threats, the vulnerabilities, and a risk level for each. A Health Check score is not that document.
  • A compliance audit at least every 12 months, and written policies, procedures, plans, and analyses.
  • A business associate's yearly written verification that it has deployed the technical safeguards, and notice within 24 hours if that associate activates its contingency plan.
  • Group health plans would put the safeguards into plan documents for their sponsors. That is a document change.
  • An AI tool that uses ePHI goes on the same inventory, under the same access and the same activity record. The proposal does not write a separate audit trail for AI.

Sources: HHS OCR fact sheet, December 27, 2024. NPRM, 90 FR 898, January 6, 2025, RIN 0945-AA22. The Unified Agenda lists final action in July 2027. Legal deadline: none.
