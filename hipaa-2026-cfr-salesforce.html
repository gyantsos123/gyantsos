Modernization of the HIPAA Security Rule · 2026

From check-the-box compliance to Active Resilience.

In 2026, federal auditors interpret HIPAA through the lens of NIST CSF 2.0 — Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. "Addressable" safeguards are now the expected legal standard: annual risk assessments, mandatory MFA, AES-256 encryption, and 72-hour recovery.
